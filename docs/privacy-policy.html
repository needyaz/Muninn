Muninn Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 12, 2026

Overview

Muninn is a clipboard manager for macOS. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data Muninn accesses and how it is handled.

Data Collection

Muninn does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app operates entirely offline with no network access.

Clipboard Data

Muninn reads the system clipboard to provide clipboard history and saved clips. All clipboard data is stored locally on your Mac in the app's sandboxed container. No clipboard content ever leaves your device.

Local Storage

Muninn stores clipboard history, saved clips, and user preferences in its Application Support directory. This data remains on your device and is not accessible to other apps or transmitted anywhere.

Analytics and Tracking

Muninn does not use any analytics, tracking, or telemetry. There are no third-party SDKs or services embedded in the app.

Third-Party Services

Muninn does not integrate with any third-party services.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the Muninn GitHub repository.
